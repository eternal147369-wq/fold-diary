Fold Diary FAQ

Why isn't my count going up on its own?

Fold Diary can't detect opens by itself. Automatic logging comes from one automation in the Shortcuts app that runs "Log an Open" every time your iPhone Duo opens.

If there's a small note under today's number saying "Not logging on its own yet", no automatic log has come in yet. Tap "Set Up →" and follow the steps.

If you've set it up and it still doesn't count, it's usually one of these:

  • The trigger isn't "iPhone Is Opened".
  • "Run Immediately" is off, so it waits for you to confirm.
  • The action isn't "Log an Open".
  • The automation is switched off. Open it in the Automation list and make sure it's on.

After fixing it, run the setup guide again. Its last page, "One Last Test", asks you to close your iPhone Duo and open it again, then tells you whether that open was logged. The Automatic Logging section on the app's main screen also shows when the last automatic log came in.

How do I set up the Shortcuts automation?

The guide opens on its own the first time you launch the app. To see it again, go to Automatic Logging on the app's main screen and tap "How to set up automatic logging". The steps:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app.
  2. Tap "Automation" at the bottom.
  3. Tap "+" in the top-right corner to add one.
  4. Scroll down and pick "iPhone Is Opened".
  5. Choose "Run Immediately" and turn off "Notify When Run".
  6. Tap "Next" → "New Blank Automation" → "Add Action", search for "Log an Open", and tap it.
  7. Tap "Done" in the top-right corner.

The app can't create the automation for you, so you do this once. It takes about a minute.

Why do I get a notification, or have to confirm, every time I open it?

In Shortcuts → Automation, open the automation and check that "Run Immediately" is selected and "Notify When Run" is off. "Log an Open" itself never opens the app.

My widget isn't showing the latest count.

The app asks the widget to redraw after every log, but iOS limits how often an app can refresh its widgets in the background each day, and Apple doesn't publish the exact number. On a day with lots of opens, the widget may lag a bit. The count inside the app is always current.

The midnight reset is scheduled ahead of time, so it isn't affected by that limit. If the widget stays stuck on one number for a long time, try removing it from the Home Screen and adding it back.

How do I fix a wrong count?

Scroll down to Fix a Count on the app's main screen. Tap the date to pick another day (today or earlier), then use − or +. A day can't go below zero.

Manual fixes are stored separately from automatic logs. In Day by Day you can see how many of a day's opens were logged by the app and how many were added or removed by hand.

What happens to my data when I get a new phone?

"Back Up to iCloud Automatically" in the Backup section of the main screen is on by default. Your records are saved to your own iCloud, so on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, reinstall the app and open it, and your records come back (right after installing it can take a moment to sync down). If this iPhone isn't signed in to iCloud, your records stay on the phone only.

You can also tap "Back Up to a File" in the Backup section and save it to the Files app or iCloud Drive, then use "Restore from a Backup" on the new phone. If both have the same day, the bigger count is kept, so nothing is counted twice.

After switching phones, also look in Shortcuts → Automation to make sure the automation is still there, and set it up again if it isn't.

If automatic backup is off and you never saved a backup file, deleting the app deletes your data, and it can't be recovered.

How do I delete my records from iCloud?

Open Settings, tap your name, then tap iCloud. Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage. If Fold Diary is in the list, tap it to delete the data it keeps in iCloud (Apple's help calls this kind of button "Delete Data from iCloud"). Apple's help doesn't promise that every app shows up in this list. If you can't find it, turn off "Back Up to iCloud Automatically" in the app so the copy stops being updated. The developer can't reach this data, so we can't delete it for you either.

Does it work on other iPhone models?

You can install it, but it's made for iPhone Duo. An iPhone that doesn't fold never opens, so there's nothing to log automatically. Only Fix a Count will be useful.

What is Import History for?

It's for people who were already logging opens with their own shortcut into a text file called duo_history.txt, so they can bring those old counts in. Most people won't need it.

  • Each block starts with a date line such as "2026年11月4日 04:00", followed by lines that say open.
  • Each block counts toward the day before its date.
  • If the app also logged that day, the larger number wins, so nothing is counted twice, not even if you import again.
  • Clear Imported Data removes everything you imported. Counts the app logged itself stay put.

How long is the free trial, and what happens when it ends?

On first launch you can tap "Start 7-Day Free Trial". Everything works during the trial.

If the 7 days pass and you haven't bought it: everything you've already logged stays, and your stats, stamps and days are still there to look through. New opens just stop being logged (the Shortcuts action, Fix a Count, Import and Restore from a Backup all pause), and the widget shows that the trial has ended. To keep logging, buy it in the app and logging comes back right away.

Will I be charged when the trial ends? Is it a subscription?

No, and no. Nothing is charged when the trial ends, and buying is up to you. The purchase is a single payment, yours to keep, and it never renews.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, reinstall the app, open the purchase screen and tap "Restore Purchases". If you had bought it, logging comes back right away. Your trial days also follow Apple's records, so reinstalling doesn't restart the trial. (Restore Purchases only restores the "bought" status, not your counts. For how your counts move to a new phone, see "What happens to my data when I get a new phone?" above.)

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles the purchase, so the developer can't refund it. Go to Apple's refund page at https://reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase and choose "Request a refund". Apple decides whether to refund it.
