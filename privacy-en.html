Fold Diary Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Developer: The developer of Fold Diary (an individual developer)

Fold Diary doesn't collect any of your data. There's no account, the app has no network features, and it contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. The developer never receives and can't see any of your data.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your daily open counts. Counts logged by the "Log an Open" action, changes you make in Fix a Count, and counts imported from a text file are stored separately.
  • The time of the most recent automatic log.
  • Two of the app's own settings: whether the setup guide has already appeared on its own, and which stamps you've already been shown as "A New Stamp".

The first two are stored in the app's App Group container, so the app, its widgets and its Shortcuts actions all read and write the same file. The third is stored in the app's own settings. All of it stays on your iPhone.

Hinge state

While the "One Last Test" page at the end of the setup guide is on screen, the app reads whether your iPhone Duo is closed or open (iOS 27.1 or later) to tell whether that open was logged. This isn't saved or sent anywhere.

Imported files and text

The app only reads a file you pick, or text you paste, in Import History. It keeps the dates and counts and doesn't keep the original file.

Shortcuts, Siri and Spotlight

The "Log an Open" and "Get Today's Count" actions are run by the Shortcuts app, Siri and Spotlight. If you use Siri, your voice is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app never receives it.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in the backup. Backups are held by Apple or your computer, and the developer can't access them.

Changing and deleting your data

You can change any day's count in Fix a Count, and remove everything you imported with Clear Imported Data. Deleting the app deletes its data, and it can't be recovered. Because your data never reaches the developer, there's nothing to request from us and nothing we could delete for you.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
