Fold Diary Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026
Developer: The developer of Fold Diary (an individual developer)

Fold Diary doesn't collect any of your data. There's no account, the app never connects to a server run by the developer, and it contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. The only things that leave your iPhone are the copy of your records saved to your own iCloud while "Back Up to iCloud Automatically" is on, and any backup file you choose to save yourself. The developer never receives and can't see any of your data.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your daily open counts. Counts logged by the "Log an Open" action (plus how many of them this iPhone logged itself), changes you make in Fix a Count, and counts imported from a text file are stored separately, along with when you last changed each day's fix or import (used to tell which side is newer when syncing).
  • The time of the most recent automatic log.
  • A few of the app's own settings: whether the setup guide has already appeared on its own, which stamps you've already been shown as "A New Stamp", whether "Back Up to iCloud Automatically" is on, when it last synced, and this iPhone's sync code (a random code used only to keep each device's own counts apart; it has nothing to do with who you are).
  • Your trial and purchase status: when your trial started and ends, whether you've bought the app, and when it last checked with the App Store.

The first, second and fourth are stored in the app's App Group container, so the app, its widgets and its Shortcuts actions all read and write the same file. The third is stored in the app's own settings. All of it is kept on your iPhone. While "Back Up to iCloud Automatically" is on, the first and second are also saved to your own iCloud (see below).

Back Up to iCloud Automatically

This switch is on by default, and you can turn it off in the Backup section of the main screen. While it's on, the app saves your daily open counts (what's described in the first item, plus each device's sync code) and the time of the most recent automatic log to the iCloud key-value storage Apple provides for this app. That copy lives in your own Apple Account and is kept by Apple under iCloud's terms and privacy policy. The developer has no server and can't read, change or delete it. Other devices signed in to the same Apple Account with this app installed read the same copy. If this iPhone isn't signed in to iCloud, your records stay on the phone only. When you turn the switch off, the app stops uploading and stops reading records back from iCloud; the copy already in iCloud stays there.

Deleting the copy in iCloud yourself

Open Settings, tap your name, then tap iCloud. Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage. If Fold Diary is in the list, tap it to delete the data it keeps in iCloud (Apple's help calls this kind of button "Delete Data from iCloud"). Apple's help doesn't promise that every app shows up in this list. If you can't find it, turn off "Back Up to iCloud Automatically" in the app so the copy stops being updated. The developer can't reach this data, so we can't delete it for you either.

Purchases and the free trial

Fold Diary is free to download, with a 7-day free trial, then a one-time purchase (not a subscription). Starting the trial, buying and restoring purchases are all handled by Apple's App Store. Your Apple Account, payment method and payment details stay with Apple, and neither the app nor the developer can receive or see them. The app only keeps the trial and purchase status described above on your iPhone, to decide whether to keep logging new opens, and never sends it to the developer. Questions about purchase history or refunds fall under Apple's privacy policy and terms.

Hinge state

While the "One Last Test" page at the end of the setup guide is on screen, the app reads whether your iPhone Duo is closed or open (iOS 27.1 or later) to tell whether that open was logged. This isn't saved or sent anywhere.

Imported files and text

The app only reads a file you pick, or text you paste, in Import History. It keeps the dates and counts and doesn't keep the original file.

Shortcuts, Siri and Spotlight

The "Log an Open" and "Get Today's Count" actions are run by the Shortcuts app, Siri and Spotlight. If you use Siri, your voice is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app never receives it.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in the backup. Backups are held by Apple or your computer, and the developer can't access them.

You can also save your records as a file from the Backup section. Where it goes (for example the Files app or iCloud Drive) is up to you, in the system's save screen. The app doesn't send this file to anyone.

Changing and deleting your data

You can change any day's count in Fix a Count, and remove everything you imported with Clear Imported Data. With automatic backup on, these changes are synced to the copy in your iCloud too. Deleting the app deletes the data on your phone. If "Back Up to iCloud Automatically" was on, the copy in your iCloud stays and comes back when you reinstall with the same Apple Account. To delete it, see "Deleting the copy in iCloud yourself" above. Backup files you saved yourself are yours to keep or delete. Because your data never reaches the developer, there's nothing to request from us and nothing we could delete for you.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
